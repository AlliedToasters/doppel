# b5.210 notes

**(1) Description.** The neuron fires on squares, mostly empty and in the middle ranks, that are *diagonal-only territory*: an enemy bishop covers the square and no other enemy piece does (including x-rays through the bishop's own pieces and the enemy bishop's own square). It also fires on squares on our queen's diagonals. Typical cases are where an enemy bishop would hit our queen or king, or where our queen or knight could land.

**(2) Evidence and what I tried.** The top examples are rays of enemy bishops, e.g. Bh6 lighting up exactly g7/g5/f8, or Bf5/Bd3 lighting up c2/b1/e4 next to a white Qd1, plus the side-to-move's queen diagonals (Qc7 → b6/a5/f4). In dev tables the strongest single feature is "the only enemy attackers are bishops": 23% fire rate against a 3% base rate, and 40% of all firings. Squares the enemy guards with any non-bishop piece almost never fire. Squares also covered by our own bishop are suppressed (0.9%). Other boosts: on our queen's open diagonal (11%), our knight attacks the square, empty square, an enemy bishop or queen x-raying through one of its own pieces, and an enemy-bishop line that also reaches our king. Activity is higher at higher ratings and early in the game (6% at ply 10–20, 0.4% after ply 80). Back ranks are quiet. I fitted the weights of these ~15 readable terms with a logistic probe on dev, then hand-rounded them. I checked interactions such as bishop × queen and bishop × knight and found they add nothing. The neuron is GELU, so tokens that are "almost on" sit at the negative dip (−0.17) and strongly-off tokens sit near 0. A raw score therefore gets a negative Spearman. I pass the score through GELU(0.5·(s−1.5)), which trades AUROC for Spearman. The raw score (RAW=True) gives Spearman −0.30, AUROC 0.918, F1 0.433.

**(3) Final dev metrics.** Spearman 0.203, AUROC 0.853, F1 0.433 (baseline concept pair F1 0.151; always-on F1 0.058).

**(4) Caveats.** About 26% of firings (mostly weak, mean 0.39) are not on any enemy-bishop or own-queen diagonal and remain unexplained. They look like further diagonal geometry, such as lines through kings or enemy queen lines behind two pieces. The rule covers several related diagonal triggers: enemy bishop control, x-rays, and our queen's diagonals. They are one geometric theme but could be two features. The mapping choice (C, K) trades Spearman against AUROC; no single scalar maximises both. Confidence: medium.
