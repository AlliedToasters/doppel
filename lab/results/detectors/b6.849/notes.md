# b6.849 notes

**(1) Description.** Fires on our own bishops (mainly developed ones) and, more diffusely, on safe "landing squares" for our long-range pieces and king - squares our bishop/queen/rook/king attack, especially where Maia expects such a piece to move - more strongly for low-rated and losing players.

**(2) Evidence and what I tried.** Our own bishop's square fires ~70-75% of the time (vs <1% for any other own piece); this is the strongest single cue (the evidence baseline `our_B & legal_from` F1 0.25). But only ~15% of firings are bishop squares; the rest are spread over ~5 squares per position, mostly empty or enemy-occupied squares attacked by our B/Q/R/K (18-28% fire rate vs 2.5% for squares none of them attack). Knight- and pawn-reach squares do not fire. Maia's top-5 destinations for B/R/Q/K moves raise firing (~30%), as does being reachable by the piece Maia wants to move. Enemy coverage (esp. pawns) and distance from our own king lower it. Position-level: firing count falls ~4x from band 700 to 2300 and rises when Stockfish says the side to move is losing; check suppresses it. X-ray diagonals, square colour and "attacked piece's escape squares" (suggested by the top examples) did not hold up as general rules. Iterations: bishop + legal-reach (F1 0.34) -> Maia B/R/Q/K destinations (0.41) -> attack/enemy-coverage/king-distance terms (0.42) -> band + eval modulators (0.45) -> reweighting (0.47). Output goes through a GELU of the rule score: the real activation is GELU, so clearly-off tokens sit near 0 and near-misses at the GELU minimum (-0.17); this raised Spearman from ~-0.05 to 0.13 at the cost of AUROC 0.87 -> 0.84 (F1 unchanged). Set `GELU = False` for the monotone version.

**(3) Final dev metrics.** Spearman 0.129 (spearman_pos 0.307), AUROC 0.837, F1 0.467 (baseline 0.251, always-on 0.114).

**(4) Caveats.** The non-bishop part is diffuse: precision/recall around 0.47 at best, and it leans on Maia's own policy (info) rather than a crisp board concept. Misses many firings in lost/low-rated positions spread over empty squares around our king and on enemy rooks/queens with no obvious rule. Spearman is low largely because of GELU's non-monotone negative region. Looks somewhat polysemantic (bishop identity vs move-target/"where my pieces can go" map, plus rating/eval gain). Confidence: medium for the bishop component, low for the landing-square component; overall low-medium.
