# b5.64 notes

1. **Description.** A rating-gated "our king's camp" neuron. It fires in strong-player games (2300 band fires 22x as often as 700) on squares one to three steps from our king and far from theirs. It favours our own knights and pawns, squares we guard, and squares hit by enemy pawns (especially advanced, storming pawns). Squares seen by the enemy queen, rook or king suppress it.

2. **Evidence and what I tried.** Fire rate by band: 0.5% / 1.6% / 4.6% / 8.1% / 10.7%. Position-level fire rate correlates r = 0.60 with band, which makes it the biggest single factor. Within a position, the fire rate against distance to our king peaks at distance 1-2 (13% overall, about 25% at high band). It falls to near 0 next to the enemy king. Being attacked by an enemy pawn adds a lot: at distance 1-3 from our king in high-band games the fire rate is 0.4-0.6, and squares hit by advanced enemy pawns fire at 49%. Top examples show this, e.g. f3/h3 lit up around Kg1 by a black pawn on g4, or f5/f6/h5/h6 around Kf4 by white g4/g5 pawns. Among occupants, our knights (43%) and pawns (25%) fire, and kings and queens almost never do. Pawns on f2/f7 next to the king fire about 47% when well defended. Squares hit by an enemy queen, rook or bishop line are suppressed. Path: logistic and per-stratum tables, then an additive score of about 20 readable terms, then residual checks. Adding our guard count, "Maia wants to move this piece" (mass_from) and the eval term helped. A step-shaped enemy-king veto and a band x spatial product did not help. A linear model on the same features reaches AUROC 0.896, and a GBM reaches 0.924, so there are interactions I did not capture.

3. **Final dev metrics** (10 scoring runs): AUROC 0.892, F1 0.390 (baseline pair 0.158), spearman_pos 0.354, overall Spearman -0.249 (raw score, no GELU).

4. **New concepts.** `advanced_enemy_pawn_attacks(p, max_rel_rank=4)`: squares attacked by enemy pawns that have reached their 4th rank or further (pressing into our half), cached per position.

5. **Caveats.** This is a graded additive score, not one crisp rule. Band plus king proximity carries most of the signal. Firings far from our king are missed: the h5/b1 promotion-area firings in a K+P ending, and e-file squares between an enemy knight and our king on e1. In opening positions at high rating, it gives false positives on our defended 3rd-4th-rank squares. It looks mildly polysemantic: king-camp defence, enemy pawn storm squares, and an endgame king-activity component. Confidence: low-medium.
